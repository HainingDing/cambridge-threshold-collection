The ante-chapel and the choir are one continuous space under the same vault; RCHME notes there is no structural division between them, so the oak screen of 1533–36 is the only separation. The central route runs from the ante-chapel through the doorway in the screen into the choir, both under the 80 ft vault.

A parallel route runs through the side chapels between the buttresses. They open into each other and into the ante-chapel through doorways, so this route passes through a series of small rooms rather than along a continuous aisle. Several are now used as exhibition rooms.
