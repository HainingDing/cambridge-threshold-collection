Built 1694–97, fitted out by 1703 and consecrated in 1704; Robert Grumbold was paid for surveying and the masonry. A screen with a central round-headed arch separates the ante-chapel from the chapel, with the organ gallery above (RCHME).

East end of the north range of Main Court.
