Begun in 1554–55 under Mary I and completed in 1567 under Elizabeth I, in Tudor Gothic. Restored by Edward Blore in 1832; Arthur Blomfield added the vestry, choir room and porch in 1868–73. Grade I listed.

North side of Great Court.
